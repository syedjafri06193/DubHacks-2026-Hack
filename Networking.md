# Networking: VP of Technology at Visa

**Who:** Syed
**Where:** Hackathon
**Goal:** Make a genuine, memorable connection and leave with a LinkedIn connection.

---

## Before You Approach

- [ ] Fill in your background and project in the intro below
- [ ] Practice the intro out loud twice
- [ ] Pick 2 Visa topics you're most curious about
- [ ] Have your LinkedIn QR code open on your phone

---

## Step 1: Introduce Yourself (about 15 seconds)

Wait for a natural pause, make eye contact, shake hands, and say your name clearly.

**Payments, fintech, AI, or security project:**
> "Hi, I'm Syed. My team is building [project] here. Since you're at Visa, I'm curious how you'd think about [fraud / AI agents making payments / security] for something like this."

**Any other project:**
> "Hi, I'm Syed. I'm working on [project] this weekend. I don't often get to talk with someone running tech at Visa's scale, so I'd love to ask you a quick question if you have a minute."

**Casual:**
> "Hi, I'm Syed. Are you judging or mentoring today? I'd love to hear what's caught your eye so far."

---

## Step 2: Break the Ice

- "What brought you out to this hackathon? Judging, mentoring, or scouting talent?"
- "Have you seen any projects so far that stood out?"
- "What would make a hackathon project interesting to someone at Visa?"

---

## Step 3: Talk Tech

Pick one or two. If he mentions something newer, ask him to explain it.

| Topic | Background | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| **Agentic commerce** | AI agents making purchases on a person's behalf (Visa Intelligent Commerce). | "How do you verify an AI agent is really authorized to spend someone's money?" |
| **AI fraud detection** | Real-time fraud scoring across billions of transactions. | "How do you balance blocking fraud against declining legitimate purchases?" |
| **Stablecoins** | Experiments settling transactions in stablecoins like USDC. | "Do you see stablecoins becoming normal infrastructure, or staying niche?" |
| **Tokenization & passkeys** | Tokens instead of card numbers, biometrics instead of passwords. | "What's the next big shift after tokenization?" |
| **Scale & reliability** | VisaNet is built so downtime is essentially not an option. | "What's it like engineering a system where downtime isn't allowed?" |

---

## Step 4: Ask for Career Advice

- "How did you get into payments technology?"
- "What problem at Visa are you most excited about right now?"
- "What skills do you wish more new engineers had?"
- "What surprised you about working at Visa's scale?"
- "If you were starting your career today, what would you focus on?"

---

## Step 5: Pitch Your Project (optional)

> "We're building [project], which [what it does] for [who it helps]. If you were judging, what would you push us on?"

Asking for feedback makes you memorable.

---

## Step 6: Wrap Up

> "Thanks so much for your time, this was really helpful. Would you mind if I connected with you on LinkedIn?"

If he seems busy:
> "I'll let you go, but could I connect with you on LinkedIn?"

---

## Step 7: Follow Up (within 24 hours)

> Hi [Name], this is Syed. We met at [hackathon name] today and talked about [topic]. I really appreciated your insight on [something he said]. I'd love to stay connected and keep learning from your work at Visa. Thanks again!

---

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Listen more than you talk | Ask for a job or referral right away |
| Follow what he gets excited about | Talk only about yourself |
| Keep it to 2–5 minutes | Overstay if he's trying to move on |
| Be curious and genuine | Recite a rehearsed pitch |
