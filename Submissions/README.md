DubHacks is UW Seattle's annual 24-hour hackathon (1,200+ hackers, $40,000+ in prizes overall). This year's theme: cooking — "Let's get cookin'!"

---

## Requirements

### What to Build
- Submit to **exactly one** title track.
- Optionally join **up to three** sponsored side tracks.
- Join **as many** non-sponsored side tracks as you want.
- Most tracks are open-ended.

### What to Submit
1. **Draft** on Devpost by **Sat 10/17, 10:30 PM**:
   - Add **all team members** as contributors (including their Discords).
   - **Declare the tracks** you're submitting to.
   - ⚠️ No draft = **no judging on Sunday**.
2. **Final submission** by **Sun 10/18, 10:00 AM**.

> Note: Some side tracks use a **video submission** instead of in-person judging — check the hacker guide.
