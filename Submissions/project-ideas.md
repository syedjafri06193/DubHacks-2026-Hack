# DubHacks '26 — Project Ideas

**Title track:** Visa: Reimagine Shopping ($5,000 cash, biggest prize)

| # | Idea | Description | Tech |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Receipt Roast** | Scan receipts; AI reviews your spending with humor, flags waste, suggests swaps | Gemini, Tiger Data, Backboard |
| 2 | **Fridge-to-Cart** ⭐ | Photo of your fridge → recipes from what you have → auto-order only missing ingredients | Gemini, Backboard |
| 3 | **Price Drop Sniper** | Watches wishlist prices and buys automatically at your target price | Tiger Data, Vultr |
| 4 | **Allergy-Safe Shopper** | Scan a product for an instant safe/unsafe verdict from your allergy profile | Gemini, Backboard |
| 5 | **Group Grocery Splitter** | Shared roommate cart; costs split by who eats what, instant settle-up | Solana, MongoDB |
| 6 | **Impulse Brake** ⭐ | Browser extension that pauses checkout and asks "do you need this?" using past regret purchases | Backboard, Gemini |
| 7 | **Local Market Map** | Find farmers markets/small vendors, compare prices to chains, pay in-app | MongoDB, Gemini |
| 8 | **Budget Meal Planner** ⭐ | Enter $40/week → full meal plan + cheapest cart across nearby stores | Gemini, Tiger Data |
| 9 | **Voice Checkout for Accessibility** | Voice + camera shopping assistant for visually impaired users | Gemini, Perceptron (check fit) |
| 10 | **Food Waste Marketplace** ⭐ | Restaurants/stores list surplus food at a discount near closing; users reserve and pay | Solana, MongoDB (Social Good) |
| 11 | **Gift Genius** | Remembers friends' interests, suggests in-budget gifts before birthdays | Backboard, Gemini |
| 12 | **Return Radar** | Tracks purchases and return windows, alerts before you lose the chance | Tiger Data, Gemini |
| 13 | **Carbon-Aware Cart** | Shows each item's climate footprint and suggests lower-impact swaps | Gemini, Tiger Data |
| 14 | **Live Shopping Stream** | Small sellers host live video sales; viewers buy instantly mid-stream | Vultr, Solana (CloudForce Most Viral?) |

## Top Picks for Visa

- **#2 Fridge-to-Cart, #8 Budget Meal Planner, #10 Food Waste Marketplace:** reinvent the shopping experience itself, fit the cooking theme, and are easy to demo.
- **#6 Impulse Brake:** fun, memorable pitch that's simple to build in 22 hours.
