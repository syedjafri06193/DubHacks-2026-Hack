## The 14 Ideas

### 1. Receipt Roast
AI roasts your spending from receipt photos, then coaches you toward better buys.
- **Visa:** in-app checkout for the cheaper swaps it suggests
- **T-Mobile:** mobile-first; scan receipts on the go
- **Perceptron:** reads crumpled, real-world receipts
- **Gemini:** writes the roast and suggests swaps
- **Backboard:** remembers your habits and past roasts
- **Tiger Data:** spending trends over time
- **Solana:** small rewards in tokens for hitting savings goals
- *Viral hook:* shareable roast cards

### 2. Fridge-to-Cart ⭐
Photograph your fridge, get recipes, and auto-order only what's missing.
- **Visa:** one-tap checkout of the missing ingredients
- **T-Mobile:** phone camera as the main input
- **Perceptron:** detects and identifies items in the fridge
- **Gemini:** generates recipes from what's detected
- **Backboard:** remembers allergies, diet, and what you liked
- **Tiger Data:** tracks pantry levels and grocery prices over time
- **Solana:** split the grocery bill with roommates instantly
- *Viral hook:* "what can I cook with this?" fridge challenges

### 3. Price Drop Sniper
Watches your wishlist and buys automatically at your target price.
- **Visa:** automated checkout at the target price
- **T-Mobile:** push alerts the moment a price drops
- **Perceptron:** snap any product in a store to add it to the watchlist
- **Gemini:** predicts whether to wait or buy now
- **Backboard:** learns your price sensitivity
- **Tiger Data:** price-history time series
- **Solana:** pooled group buys that unlock bulk pricing

### 4. Allergy-Safe Shopper
Scan a product for an instant safe/unsafe verdict.
- **Visa:** buy safe alternatives in-app
- **T-Mobile:** works in-store on your phone
- **Perceptron:** reads ingredient labels from the package
- **Gemini:** interprets the ingredients against your allergies
- **Backboard:** stores your allergy profile and family members' profiles
- **Tiger Data:** logs scans and reactions over time
- **Solana:** verified-safe product attestations on-chain

### 5. Group Grocery Splitter
Roommates share one cart; costs split by who eats what.
- **Visa:** single group checkout
- **T-Mobile:** shared mobile cart with live updates
- **Perceptron:** scan the receipt to assign items to people
- **Gemini:** suggests meals that use overlapping ingredients
- **Backboard:** remembers each roommate's preferences
- **Tiger Data:** household spending dashboard
- **Solana:** instant settle-up between roommates

### 6. Impulse Brake ⭐
Pauses checkout and asks "do you really need this?"
- **Visa:** sits on top of the checkout step
- **T-Mobile:** mobile version that intercepts shopping apps
- **Perceptron:** in-store, snap an item to get a "should I?" check
- **Gemini:** writes a personalized nudge
- **Backboard:** remembers past regret purchases
- **Tiger Data:** money-saved tracker
- **Solana:** saved money moves into a savings wallet
- *Viral hook:* "money saved this month" share cards

### 7. Local Market Map
Find small vendors and farmers markets, compare against chains, pay in-app.
- **Visa:** pay vendors in-app
- **T-Mobile:** location-based mobile discovery
- **Perceptron:** snap produce to identify it and compare prices
- **Gemini:** compares quality and price
- **Backboard:** remembers favorite vendors
- **Tiger Data:** market price trends
- **Solana:** low-fee payments for small vendors

### 8. Budget Meal Planner ⭐
Enter $40 a week, get a meal plan and the cheapest cart.
- **Visa:** buy the planned cart in one tap
- **T-Mobile:** mobile meal-plan and shopping list
- **Perceptron:** scan your pantry so the plan avoids duplicates
- **Gemini:** generates the meal plan within the budget
- **Backboard:** learns your tastes week to week
- **Tiger Data:** budget vs. actual spending
- **Solana:** micro-rewards for staying under budget

### 9. Voice Checkout for Accessibility
Voice and camera shopping assistant for visually impaired users.
- **Visa:** accessible, voice-confirmed checkout
- **T-Mobile:** phone-based, works anywhere in a store
- **Perceptron:** describes shelves, products, and prices aloud
- **Gemini:** conversational voice assistant
- **Backboard:** remembers usual items and store layouts
- **Tiger Data:** purchase history for fast reorders
- **Solana:** caregiver-approved spending wallets

### 10. Food Waste Marketplace ⭐
Stores list surplus food at a discount near closing time.
- **Visa:** reserve and pay in-app
- **T-Mobile:** real-time nearby deals on your phone
- **Perceptron:** sellers photograph surplus to list it instantly
- **Gemini:** writes listings and sets discount prices
- **Backboard:** remembers buyer preferences for alerts
- **Tiger Data:** food-saved and waste-reduction metrics
- **Solana:** instant, low-fee payouts to small restaurants

### 11. Gift Genius
Remembers friends' interests and suggests in-budget gifts.
- **Visa:** one-tap gift purchase
- **T-Mobile:** reminders before birthdays
- **Perceptron:** snap something your friend would love to save it
- **Gemini:** generates gift ideas
- **Backboard:** remembers each friend's interests over time
- **Tiger Data:** gift spending by person and month
- **Solana:** group gift pooling

### 12. Return Radar
Tracks purchases and warns you before return windows close.
- **Visa:** purchase-linked tracking and refund status
- **T-Mobile:** deadline push alerts
- **Perceptron:** reads receipts and product condition from photos
- **Gemini:** parses store return policies
- **Backboard:** remembers what you tend to return
- **Tiger Data:** return deadlines as a timeline
- **Solana:** resell items you missed the window on

### 13. Carbon-Aware Cart
Shows each item's climate footprint and suggests swaps.
- **Visa:** checkout with the greener cart
- **T-Mobile:** scan items in-store
- **Perceptron:** recognizes products from photos
- **Gemini:** estimates footprint and suggests swaps
- **Backboard:** remembers your sustainability goals
- **Tiger Data:** your footprint over time
- **Solana:** green rewards for low-impact carts

### 14. Live Shopping Stream
Small sellers host live video sales; viewers buy mid-stream.
- **Visa:** instant purchase during the stream
- **T-Mobile:** mobile live streaming
- **Perceptron:** detects products on camera and pins them as buy buttons
- **Gemini:** live captions and product descriptions
- **Backboard:** remembers what each viewer likes
- **Tiger Data:** live sales analytics
- **Solana:** instant seller payouts
- *Viral hook:* built for sharing; strong for CloudForce Most Viral

## Reality Check

Integrating 13 tracks in about 22 hours is a lot. Track Applicability is 30% of the score, and judges reward depth over checkboxes. Build the core loop first (for #2: photo → detect → recipe → cart → checkout), then add integrations in this order:

1. Gemini + Perceptron (core)
2. Visa checkout (title track)
3. Backboard memory
4. Tiger Data dashboard
5. Solana payments
6. Vultr hosting + GoDaddy domain
7. After submitting: DEV post + Commit Fellowship form
